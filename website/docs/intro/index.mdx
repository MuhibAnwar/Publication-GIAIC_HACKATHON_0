# Introduction to Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics

## Welcome to Physical AI

Welcome to the fascinating field of **Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics**! This textbook bridges the gap between the digital brain and the physical body, focusing on embodied intelligence that operates in physical space.

<div className="definition-box">

### Key Insight: The Physical AI Revolution
Physical AI represents a significant transition from AI models confined to digital environments to intelligent systems that understand and interact with the physical world. As we advance toward more capable robotic systems, the integration of high-level AI (Large Language Models, Vision-Language-Action models) with low-level control systems (Actuators, ROS2) becomes increasingly important.

</div>

## The Concept of Embodied Intelligence

<div className="theorem-box">

### Core Principle: Embodied Intelligence
Embodied intelligence refers to intelligence that emerges from the interaction between an agent's cognitive processes, its body, and its environment. Unlike traditional AI systems that operate purely in digital spaces, embodied systems must understand and navigate the complexities of physical laws.

</div>

This concept is crucial for humanoid robotics because these robots share our physical form and can be trained with abundant data from interacting in human environments. The physical embodiment introduces unique challenges and opportunities that pure digital AI systems don't face.

## Learning Approach

This textbook adopts a **project-based learning approach**. You will progressively build toward a final capstone project where you'll implement a simulated humanoid robot capable of receiving voice commands, planning paths, navigating obstacles, identifying objects, and manipulating them.

<div className="exercise-box">

### Capstone Project Preview
Throughout the course, you'll work on a cohesive project that culminates in a humanoid robot system. This project will demonstrate your understanding of all components:

- Voice command processing and cognitive planning
- Path planning for bipedal locomotion
- Object detection and manipulation
- Multi-sensor fusion for state estimation

</div>

Throughout the course, we'll use industry-standard tools including **ROS 2** for robot control, **NVIDIA Isaac** for perception systems, and various simulation environments to provide a comprehensive learning experience.
